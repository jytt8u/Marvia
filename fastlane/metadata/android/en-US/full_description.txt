Marvia is an Android client for connecting to VPN servers using an access key or subscription URL. You need your own node or a configuration from its operator. The app does not provide servers or sell access.

Features:
• Marvia subscriptions and keys for VP1, VLESS, VMess, Trojan, Shadowsocks, Hysteria 2 and WireGuard.
• Import links and QR codes from the camera or gallery; scanning works without Google services.
• Automatic node selection and failover to an available alternative; manual server selection.
• Android's system VPN interface, a Quick Settings tile and optional startup after reboot.
• Select apps that use the tunnel or connect directly; routing, DNS and DNS-over-HTTPS settings.
• Local traffic, speed, session and diagnostic data; background subscription updates and reminders subject to Android restrictions.
• English and Russian interface.

VP1 uses Noise with X25519 and ChaCha20-Poly1305 over TLS/REALITY, WebSocket or QUIC. It is a separate protocol and is not compatible with WireGuard or Xray clients. Third-party protocols use their own keys.

Requirements: Android 7.0 or later, ARM64 or ARMv7. The project is in alpha; testing on physical devices and networks is ongoing. Connectivity depends on the server, configuration and network.

There are no advertising or analytics SDKs. Keys, settings, logs and session history stay on the device. The panel receives subscription requests and optional node availability reports; see docs/privacy.md in the repository for details. License: AGPL-3.0.
